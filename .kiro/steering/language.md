# Language policy

- All code, identifiers, comments, docstrings, documentation, commit messages and PR text: **English**.
- Customer-facing conversation (prompts' output, templates, demo scripts, test utterances): **Spanish and Portuguese**.
- Spanish must cover Mexican, Colombian and Argentine variants.
- The dataset is Spanish-only; Portuguese data is team-generated/translated and must be labeled with its provenance.
- Every customer-facing template exists in both `es` and `pt`; a missing translation fails the tests.
